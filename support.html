Frequently asked questions

How do I log a cigarette quickly?

Tap +1 on the Today screen. You can also add the Ember widget to your Home or Lock Screen, add the Ember control to Control Center or the Action button, or say "Log a cigarette in Ember" to Siri. In Ember, open Settings › Quick logging › Widgets, Control Center & Siri for a step-by-step guide.

I logged one by mistake.

Tap Undo straight after logging, or open History, tap an entry to edit it, or swipe it to delete.

How does the taper plan work?

You choose a starting daily limit and how often it should drop (every week, 2 weeks or month). Ember lowers your limit step by step and shows a gentle pace for the day. You can change or restart the plan any time in Settings.

My data isn't syncing between devices.

  • Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is on.
  • Check that Ember is allowed to use iCloud: Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Apps Using iCloud.
  • Open Ember on each device while on Wi-Fi. Syncing can take a few minutes.

The widget says "Open Ember".

After an update, Ember needs to be opened once to finish updating its data. Open the app and the widget refreshes.

The Live Activity doesn't appear.

Turn on Show on Lock Screen in Ember › Settings › Quick logging, and make sure Live Activities are allowed in iPhone Settings › Ember.

How do I export or delete my data?

Ember › Settings › Your data › Export as CSV or Delete all data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Ember medical advice?

No. Ember helps you notice and reduce smoking. It isn't a medical device. Health milestones are general information from the WHO and the NHS. For help quitting, talk to a healthcare professional or a local quit line, for example:

  • US: 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) · smokefree.gov
  • UK: NHS Quit Smoking
  • India: National Tobacco Quitline 1800-11-2356
  • Elsewhere: WHO quitting toolkit
