Frequently asked questions

How do I log a cigarette quickly?

Tap +1 on the Today screen. You can also add the Ebb widget to your Home or Lock Screen, add the Ebb control to Control Center or the Action button, or say "Log a cigarette in Ebb" to Siri. In Ebb, open Settings › Quick logging › Widgets, Control Center & Siri for a step-by-step guide.

On Android: add the Ebb widget to your Home screen, add the Log cigarette tile to Quick Settings, or touch and hold the Ebb icon for shortcuts. Settings › Quick logging › Widgets, Quick Settings & shortcuts can add them for you.

I logged one by mistake.

Tap Undo straight after logging, or open History, tap an entry to edit it, or swipe it to delete.

How does the taper plan work?

You choose a starting daily limit and how often it should drop (every week, 2 weeks or month). Ebb lowers your limit step by step and shows a gentle pace for the day. You can change or restart the plan any time in Settings.

My data isn't syncing between devices.

  • Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is on.
  • Check that Ebb is allowed to use iCloud: Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Apps Using iCloud.
  • Open Ebb on each device while on Wi-Fi. Syncing can take a few minutes.

I got a new Android phone. Where's my data?

Ebb is included in Android's backup to your Google account. Restore from that backup when you set up the new phone, then open Ebb. The iPhone and Android apps don't sync with each other; use Export as CSV to keep a copy.

Reminders don't arrive on Android.

Allow notifications for Ebb in Android Settings › Apps › Ebb › Notifications. Some phones pause apps in the background to save battery: if reminders are late, set Ebb's battery use to Unrestricted.

The widget says "Open Ebb".

After an update, Ebb needs to be opened once to finish updating its data. Open the app and the widget refreshes.

The Live Activity doesn't appear.

Turn on Lock Screen card after logging in Ebb › Settings › Quick logging, and make sure Live Activities are allowed in iPhone Settings › Ebb. On Android the card is a notification: allow notifications for Ebb, and make sure the Lock Screen card notification category isn't turned off.

How do I export or delete my data?

Ebb › Settings › Your data › Export as CSV or Delete all data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Ebb medical advice?

No. Ebb helps you notice and reduce smoking. It isn't a medical device. Health milestones are general information from the WHO and the NHS. For help quitting, talk to a healthcare professional or a local quit line, for example:

  • US: 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) · smokefree.gov
  • UK: NHS Quit Smoking
  • India: National Tobacco Quitline 1800-11-2356
  • Elsewhere: WHO quitting toolkit
