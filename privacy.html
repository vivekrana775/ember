🔥 Ebb

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: Ebb doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers. What you log stays on your device and in your own private backup: iCloud on iPhone, your Google account backup on Android. We can't see it.

What Ebb stores

To work, Ebb stores the information you enter:

  • The cigarettes you log (time, plus an optional trigger such as "coffee" or "stress").
  • Cravings you log in Craving SOS (time, optional strength and trigger, and whether you smoked).
  • Your settings: daily goal, taper plan, pack price and currency, and your personal reason for cutting down.

This information is stored on your device, in storage shared only with Ebb's own widgets. The Android app doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

iCloud sync (iPhone)

If you're signed in to iCloud, Ebb syncs your data between your devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple Account. The developer can't access it. Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy. To turn sync off, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud on your iPhone.

Google backup (Android)

On Android, Ebb's data and settings are included in Android's own backup to your Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone. The backup is made by Android, is encrypted with your phone's screen lock, and the developer can't access it. To turn it off, go to Settings › Google › Backup on your phone. The iPhone and Android apps don't sync with each other.

What Ebb doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising or tracking.
  • Your data isn't sold, shared or sent to any server run by the developer.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Health data.

Notifications

Optional reminders (evening check-in, health milestones and pace nudges) are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

Exporting and deleting your data

You control your data. In Ebb › Settings › Your data you can:

  • Export as CSV. This creates a file that you choose where to share. Ebb sends it nowhere by itself.
  • Delete all data. This removes your log from the device, and from your iCloud copy or your next Google backup.

Deleting the app removes data stored on the device. Data in iCloud can also be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. On Android, old backups can be removed from Google Drive › Backups.

Links to other sites

Ebb links to public health information (for example from the World Health Organization). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Ebb isn't directed at children and is rated for adults on the App Store and Google Play. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
