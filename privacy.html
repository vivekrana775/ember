Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: Ember doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers. What you log stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. We can't see it.

What Ember stores

To work, Ember stores the information you enter:

  • The cigarettes you log (time, plus an optional trigger such as "coffee" or "stress").
  • Cravings you log in Craving SOS (time, optional strength and trigger, and whether you smoked).
  • Your settings: daily goal, taper plan, pack price and currency, and your personal reason for cutting down.

This information is stored on your device, in storage shared only with Ember's own widgets.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Ember syncs your data between your devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple Account. The developer can't access it. Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy. To turn sync off, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud on your iPhone.

What Ember doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising or tracking.
  • Your data isn't sold, shared or sent to any server run by the developer.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Health data.

Notifications

Optional reminders (evening check-in, health milestones and pace nudges) are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

Exporting and deleting your data

You control your data. In Ember › Settings › Your data you can:

  • Export as CSV. This creates a file that you choose where to share. Ember sends it nowhere by itself.
  • Delete all data. This removes your log from the device and from your iCloud copy.

Deleting the app removes data stored on the device. Data in iCloud can also be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Links to other sites

Ember links to public health information (for example from the World Health Organization). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Ember isn't directed at children and is rated for adults on the App Store. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
