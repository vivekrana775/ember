🔥 Ember

Smoke less, at your own pace.

A simple, private iPhone app for noticing your habit, cutting down gently and celebrating every step.

  • One-tap logging from the app, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, the Action button or Siri.
  • A gentle taper plan that lowers your daily limit at a pace you choose.
  • Craving SOS: a guided breathing pause and a reminder of your own reasons.
  • Insights into when and why you smoke.
  • Wins: money saved, cigarettes avoided and health milestones.
  • Private by design: no accounts, ads or tracking.
